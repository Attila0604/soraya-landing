Questions

Good to know

Is Soraya free?

Yes. You can see your Sun and Moon sign right away, without an account. Birth chart, personal daily horoscope, partner match and chat with Soraya come with a free account.

Do I need my birth time?

No, but it makes the reading more precise: with time and place Soraya also calculates your ascendant and houses.

What happens to my data?

Soraya uses your birth details only for your calculations. You can delete your account with all data at any time.

How are the readings created?

First, Soraya calculates the planetary positions at your birth with astronomical precision (Swiss Ephemeris). From that, an AI writes your personal reading. The texts are meant as an impulse for self-reflection – not as medical, legal or financial advice.

Is Soraya available for iPhone?

Yes. Open Soraya in Safari and tap “Share” → “Add to Home Screen” – it then starts like an app.
